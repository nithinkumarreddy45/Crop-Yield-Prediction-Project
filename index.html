    Comprehensive Pest & Disease Advisory

    ICAR / NPSS Data
    High Alert

    Fall Armyworm (Maize)

    Expected severe infestation due to recent weather shifts.

    Action: Spray Spinetoram 11.7% SC or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC immediately at early vegetative stages.

    High Alert

    Pink Bollworm (Cotton)

    Major threat during the boll formation stage. Can cause up to 30% yield loss.

    Action: Install Pheromone traps (5/acre). If catch exceeds 8 moths/day, spray Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG.

    High Alert

    Yellow Stem Borer (Rice/Paddy)

    Causes 'dead heart' in vegetative stage and 'white ear' in reproductive stage.

    Action: Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10kg/acre in standing water or spray Chlorantraniliprole.

    Watch

    Yellow Rust (Wheat)

    Cool temperatures and morning dew heavily favor yellow rust formation.

    Action: Monitor lower leaves. Apply Propiconazole 25 EC (1ml/L) as soon as yellow stripes become visible.

    Watch

    Late Blight (Potato & Tomato)

    High humidity and cloudy weather are triggering rapid fungal spread.

    Action: Preventative spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g/L. Ensure proper field drainage.

    Watch

    Whitefly (Cotton & Vegetables)

    Acts as a vector for Leaf Curl Virus. High multiplication rate in dry spells.

    Action: Place yellow sticky traps. Spray Neem oil (1500 ppm) or Diafenthiuron 50 WP if infestation is high.

    General Advisory

    Root Grub (Sugarcane)

    Damages root systems causing yellowing and lodging of canes.

    Action: Soil application of Metarhizium anisopliae or drench roots with Chlorpyriphos 20 EC.

    General Advisory

    Pod Borer / Gram Caterpillar (Chana)

    Major pest targeting the crop during the crucial flowering and podding stages.

    Action: Setup bird perches. Spray Indoxacarb 14.5 SC or Flubendiamide if 1 larva per meter row is seen.
